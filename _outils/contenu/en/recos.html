Off campus

The travels and books that have stayed with me, made me think or simply made me feel good.

Travels

  • Peru 2026

    Full of wonderful, generous people and breathtaking landscapes, Peru is sure to win your heart (but be ready to walk!).

  • Italy 2025

    Discovering the French-speaking community of the Aosta Valley and the magnificent Alps.

  • France 2025

    Paris, Versailles, Mont-Saint-Michel and the Vendée.

  • Morocco 2024

    A magical journey into the Sahara Desert, the Atlas Mountains and everything Morocco had to offer.

  • Greece 2023

    Discovering the greatest philosophers of antiquity and the Cyclades islands.

Books

  • The Robot series Isaac Asimov

    A visionary, Asimov introduces his famous Three Laws of Robotics and leads us to reflect, ahead of his time, on the issues raised by robots.

  • The Einstein Enigma José Rodrigues dos Santos

    A great adventure, steeped in science, that makes us question our perception of religion.

  • The Plouffe Family Roger Lemelin

    Lemelin paints a funny yet so real portrait of a family from Quebec City's Lower Town in the 1930s and 1940s, one you fall in love with on every page.
