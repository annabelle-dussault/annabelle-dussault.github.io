Books

  • The Robot series

    Isaac Asimov

    A visionary, Asimov introduces his famous Three Laws of Robotics and leads us to reflect, ahead of his time, on the issues raised by robots.

  • The Einstein Enigma

    José Rodrigues dos Santos

    A great adventure, steeped in science, that makes us question our perception of religion.

  • The Plouffe Family

    Roger Lemelin

    Lemelin paints a funny yet so real portrait of a family from Quebec City's Lower Town in the 1930s and 1940s, one you fall in love with on every page.
