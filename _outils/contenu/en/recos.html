Travels

My album of adventures: discovering the world, one country at a time.

Peru 2026

From the Andes to Machu Picchu, breathtaking landscapes and wonderfully generous people. A guaranteed favourite, as long as you like to walk!

Italy 2025

From the Aosta Valley to the foot of Mont Blanc, discovering a little-known French-speaking community in the heart of the Alps.

France 2025

From Paris to the Vendée, by way of Versailles and Mont-Saint-Michel, a journey through the history of France.

Morocco 2024

From the Sahara Desert to the Atlas Mountains, a magical journey through a country of a thousand colours.

Greece 2023

From Athens to the Cyclades islands, in the footsteps of the great philosophers of Antiquity.

Books

The books that have stayed with me over the years. The list will only keep growing!

  • The Robot series

    Isaac Asimov, 1950

    In this book, Asimov introduces the famous three laws of robotics and leads us to reflect, ahead of his time, on the issues raised by robots.

  • The Einstein Enigma

    José Rodrigues dos Santos, 2006

    A great adventure, steeped in physics and mathematics, that makes us question our perception of religion.

  • The Plouffe Family

    Roger Lemelin, 1948

    Lemelin paints a funny yet so real portrait of a family from Quebec City's Lower Town in the 1930s and 1940s, one you fall in love with on every page.
