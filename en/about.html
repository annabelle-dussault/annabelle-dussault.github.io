About me

I am a master's student in political science (thesis track) at Université Laval, after completing a Bachelor of Laws.

Passionate and enthusiastic, I am an avid reader and a traveller at heart. You will find some of my favourite books and trips in the Off campus section.

I am a research assistant at the Chaire de leadership en enseignement des sciences sociales numériques (CLESSN) and at the Canada Research Chair in International Political Economy. I was also a page at the National Assembly of Québec and a student researcher at the Court of Appeal of Quebec.

Since 2023, I have been involved in the Quebec Youth Parliament, where I currently serve as Minister of Democratic Institutions. I am also a board member of the Association québécoise des jeunes parlementaires.

Interests

  • Democratic institutions and parliamentary life
  • International relations and global governance
  • International political economy
  • Law and public policy
